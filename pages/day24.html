Training Summary

The final session reviewed the major topics covered during the four-week training: strategic games, payoff matrices, the Prisoner's Dilemma, Nash equilibrium, Matching Pennies, the Divorce Problem, mixed strategies, expected payoff, mechanism design, cake cutting, fairness, voting, auctions, bargaining, and incentive compatibility. We summarized key methods and reflected on how these ideas can be applied to practical problems.

Learning Objectives / Outcomes

Summarize the main concepts and select an appropriate method for analyzing a strategic or allocation problem.

Learning Reflection

The training developed my understanding of strategic decision-making and incentive-based system design. I gained practice in representing problems, evaluating strategies, and reasoning about fair or efficient outcomes.

Key Takeaways

  • Connect the day's concepts with strategic decision-making.
  • Use clear assumptions and structured reasoning when analyzing examples.
  • Review the topic and practice a related problem independently.
