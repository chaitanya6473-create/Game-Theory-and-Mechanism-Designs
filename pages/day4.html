Reading and Constructing Payoff Matrices

Daily training diary entry

Student NameChaitanya
Date2 July, 2026
Training TopicReading and Constructing Payoff Matrices
Week1 of 4

Training Summary

The focus was on payoff matrices. We practiced placing each player's strategies along the rows and columns and recording the resulting payoffs in each cell. Several small examples were analyzed to understand how a single strategy choice can affect both participants. We also checked that the order of payoffs was clearly defined.

Learning Objectives / Outcomes

Construct and interpret a two-player payoff matrix without confusing players or payoff order.

Learning Reflection

Payoff matrices make strategic interactions easier to compare. I became more comfortable reading outcomes from a table and explaining what each cell means.

Key Takeaways

  • Connect the day's concepts with strategic decision-making.
  • Use clear assumptions and structured reasoning when analyzing examples.
  • Review the topic and practice a related problem independently.
