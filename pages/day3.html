Training Summary

We compared normal-form games with extensive-form games and discussed simultaneous versus sequential decisions. The session introduced the use of payoff tables for compactly representing strategic choices and game trees for showing decision order, possible actions, and outcomes. We also discussed the difference between zero-sum and non-zero-sum games.

Learning Objectives / Outcomes

Choose an appropriate representation for a strategic situation and distinguish common game classifications.

Learning Reflection

I learned that the representation depends on whether decisions happen simultaneously or sequentially and on what information players have.

Key Takeaways

  • Connect the day's concepts with strategic decision-making.
  • Use clear assumptions and structured reasoning when analyzing examples.
  • Review the topic and practice a related problem independently.
