Training Summary

The first two weeks' concepts were consolidated through practice problems involving payoff matrices, dominant strategies, Nash equilibrium, the Prisoner's Dilemma, and Matching Pennies. We compared solution methods and discussed common mistakes, such as checking only one opponent action or confusing a best response with a dominant strategy.

Learning Objectives / Outcomes

Apply the foundational solution concepts to unfamiliar two-player games.

Learning Reflection

Revision helped identify which steps should be followed consistently: define strategies, inspect payoffs, find best responses, and then test stability.

Key Takeaways

  • Connect the day's concepts with strategic decision-making.
  • Use clear assumptions and structured reasoning when analyzing examples.
  • Review the topic and practice a related problem independently.
