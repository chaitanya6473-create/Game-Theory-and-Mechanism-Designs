Training Summary

The Cake Cutting problem was introduced as a fair-division setting in which participants may value different portions of a shared resource. We discussed proportionality, envy-freeness, and the challenge of making a division fair when preferences are private. The session explored simple procedures such as one person cutting and another choosing, along with their assumptions and limitations.

Learning Objectives / Outcomes

Define basic fairness criteria and explain how a cake-cutting procedure can address differing valuations.

Learning Reflection

The example made abstract fairness ideas concrete. I understood that a procedure can be designed to encourage participants to reveal or act on their preferences strategically.

Key Takeaways

  • Connect the day's concepts with strategic decision-making.
  • Use clear assumptions and structured reasoning when analyzing examples.
  • Review the topic and practice a related problem independently.
