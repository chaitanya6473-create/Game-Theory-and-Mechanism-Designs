Training Summary

Incentive compatibility was introduced as a property of a mechanism in which participants benefit from reporting their information truthfully, according to the mechanism's rules. We discussed why private information creates strategic reporting problems and how a well-designed mechanism can align incentives with accurate information.

Learning Objectives / Outcomes

Explain incentive compatibility and identify the strategic reporting issue it is intended to address.

Learning Reflection

I learned that asking participants to be honest is not enough; the mechanism should make truthful behavior strategically sensible.

Key Takeaways

  • Connect the day's concepts with strategic decision-making.
  • Use clear assumptions and structured reasoning when analyzing examples.
  • Review the topic and practice a related problem independently.
