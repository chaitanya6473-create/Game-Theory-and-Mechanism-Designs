Training Summary

The session covered bargaining situations where participants negotiate over a surplus or shared resource. We discussed disagreement outcomes, bargaining power, and how the available alternatives affect negotiation. Simple examples were used to compare possible agreements and identify the role of incentives in reaching a deal.

Learning Objectives / Outcomes

Analyze a basic bargaining situation using preferences, feasible agreements, and disagreement outcomes.

Learning Reflection

The discussion showed that negotiation outcomes depend not only on what each party wants but also on what happens if no agreement is reached.

Key Takeaways

  • Connect the day's concepts with strategic decision-making.
  • Use clear assumptions and structured reasoning when analyzing examples.
  • Review the topic and practice a related problem independently.
