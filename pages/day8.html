Training Summary

We analyzed Matching Pennies, where one player wins when the coins match and the other wins when they differ. The game illustrates strategic unpredictability and the absence of a pure-strategy equilibrium. We discussed why predictable behavior can be exploited and how randomization can be useful in competitive settings.

Learning Objectives / Outcomes

Explain the structure of Matching Pennies and why players need mixed strategies.

Learning Reflection

The game demonstrated that a player may need to randomize rather than consistently choose one action. This was a useful example of strategic thinking under competition.

Key Takeaways

  • Connect the day's concepts with strategic decision-making.
  • Use clear assumptions and structured reasoning when analyzing examples.
  • Review the topic and practice a related problem independently.
