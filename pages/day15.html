Training Summary

We compared fairness properties used in allocation problems, including proportionality and envy-freeness, and discussed why different criteria may conflict or be difficult to satisfy simultaneously. Examples illustrated how an allocation rule can be evaluated based on the preferences and claims of the participants.

Learning Objectives / Outcomes

Compare fairness criteria and assess an allocation using a stated fairness property.

Learning Reflection

I learned to state clearly which definition of fairness is being used instead of assuming that one division is fair in every sense.

Key Takeaways

  • Connect the day's concepts with strategic decision-making.
  • Use clear assumptions and structured reasoning when analyzing examples.
  • Review the topic and practice a related problem independently.
