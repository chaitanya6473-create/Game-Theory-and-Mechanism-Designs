Training Summary

We reviewed expected payoff as the probability-weighted average of possible outcomes. Worked examples showed how to calculate expected values for mixed strategies and compare alternatives under uncertainty. The session also distinguished expected monetary payoff from broader utility, which can reflect preferences beyond money.

Learning Objectives / Outcomes

Calculate expected payoff from a probability distribution and use it to compare strategies.

Learning Reflection

The calculations helped connect probability with strategic decision-making. I practiced checking that probabilities sum to one and interpreting the resulting expected value.

Key Takeaways

  • Connect the day's concepts with strategic decision-making.
  • Use clear assumptions and structured reasoning when analyzing examples.
  • Review the topic and practice a related problem independently.
