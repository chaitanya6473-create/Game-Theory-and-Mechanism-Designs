Training Summary

The session covered the basic components of a game: players, available strategies, outcomes, and payoffs. We examined how a payoff represents the benefit or cost associated with an outcome and how a player's payoff may depend on the strategy profile chosen by everyone. Simple examples were used to distinguish actions from outcomes.

Learning Objectives / Outcomes

Describe a game using its players, strategy sets, and payoff functions.

Learning Reflection

Understanding the basic vocabulary made it easier to read and construct game models. I practiced identifying the players and possible choices in everyday examples.

Key Takeaways

  • Connect the day's concepts with strategic decision-making.
  • Use clear assumptions and structured reasoning when analyzing examples.
  • Review the topic and practice a related problem independently.
