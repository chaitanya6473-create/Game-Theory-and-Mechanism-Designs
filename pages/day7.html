Nash Equilibrium

Week2 of 4

Training Summary

The session introduced Nash equilibrium as a strategy profile in which no player can improve their payoff by changing their own strategy alone while the other players keep theirs fixed. We identified best responses in payoff matrices and used them to locate pure-strategy Nash equilibria. Examples also showed that some games may have multiple equilibria or none in pure strategies.

Learning Objectives / Outcomes

Find best responses and test whether a strategy profile is a Nash equilibrium.

Learning Reflection

The concept clarified what it means for a set of choices to be stable, while also showing that equilibrium does not necessarily mean the outcome is socially optimal.

Key Takeaways

  • Connect the day's concepts with strategic decision-making.
  • Use clear assumptions and structured reasoning when analyzing examples.
  • Review the topic and practice a related problem independently.
