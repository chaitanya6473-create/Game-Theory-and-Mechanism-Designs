Training Summary

The session formalized mixed strategies as probability distributions over a player's available pure strategies. We discussed how randomization can make a player's behavior harder to predict and how equilibrium probabilities can be selected to keep an opponent indifferent between relevant actions. Simple two-action examples were used to practice the idea.

Learning Objectives / Outcomes

Interpret a mixed strategy and explain the role of indifference in choosing equilibrium probabilities.

Learning Reflection

I learned that randomization can be a rational strategic choice, not simply a lack of planning. The key is to choose probabilities that prevent the opponent from gaining an advantage.

Key Takeaways

  • Connect the day's concepts with strategic decision-making.
  • Use clear assumptions and structured reasoning when analyzing examples.
  • Review the topic and practice a related problem independently.
