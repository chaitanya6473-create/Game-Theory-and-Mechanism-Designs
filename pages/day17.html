Training Summary

We introduced auctions as mechanisms for allocating goods and collecting payments. The session compared first-price and second-price sealed-bid auctions and discussed how bidders' incentives differ across formats. We considered the relationship between private valuations, bidding strategies, and auction rules.

Learning Objectives / Outcomes

Distinguish common auction formats and explain how rules influence bidding incentives.

Learning Reflection

I learned that the best bidding strategy depends on the mechanism. Auction design can influence both the allocation and the payments.

Key Takeaways

  • Connect the day's concepts with strategic decision-making.
  • Use clear assumptions and structured reasoning when analyzing examples.
  • Review the topic and practice a related problem independently.
