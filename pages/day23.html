Training Summary

A set of integrated exercises combined game-theoretic analysis with mechanism-design ideas. We identified players and preferences, represented strategic choices, analyzed best responses or bargaining options, and then considered how changing the rules could improve the outcome. The exercises encouraged clear assumptions and step-by-step reasoning.

Learning Objectives / Outcomes

Analyze a problem from model construction through solution and discuss possible rule changes.

Learning Reflection

I practiced combining concepts rather than treating each topic independently. Stating assumptions clearly made the analysis more reliable.

Key Takeaways

  • Connect the day's concepts with strategic decision-making.
  • Use clear assumptions and structured reasoning when analyzing examples.
  • Review the topic and practice a related problem independently.
