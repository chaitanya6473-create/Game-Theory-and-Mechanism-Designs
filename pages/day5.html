Training Summary

The Prisoner's Dilemma was studied as a classic example of conflict between individual incentives and collective benefit. We analyzed the cooperate and defect choices, compared the payoffs, and discussed why each player may have an incentive to defect even though mutual cooperation can produce a better joint outcome. The session also introduced the idea of a social dilemma.

Learning Objectives / Outcomes

Explain the strategic tension in the Prisoner's Dilemma and identify its dominant-strategy outcome.

Learning Reflection

The example showed that individually rational choices do not always lead to the best collective result. It highlighted the importance of incentives and trust.

Key Takeaways

  • Connect the day's concepts with strategic decision-making.
  • Use clear assumptions and structured reasoning when analyzing examples.
  • Review the topic and practice a related problem independently.
