Training Summary

The session introduced security games involving defenders and attackers with competing objectives. We discussed how limited resources must be allocated across targets and how strategic uncertainty affects planning. The examples emphasized anticipating an opponent's incentives rather than protecting every location equally.

Learning Objectives / Outcomes

Describe the defender-attacker structure and explain why resource allocation depends on strategic behavior.

Learning Reflection

I learned that security planning can benefit from modeling likely responses and making coverage less predictable.

Key Takeaways

  • Connect the day's concepts with strategic decision-making.
  • Use clear assumptions and structured reasoning when analyzing examples.
  • Review the topic and practice a related problem independently.
