Training Summary

The training moved from analyzing games to designing rules that shape strategic behavior. Mechanism design was introduced as the study of how to create institutions, procedures, or systems so that participants' incentives support a desired outcome. We discussed the roles of outcomes, messages or reports, and rules that map reports to decisions.

Learning Objectives / Outcomes

Explain the difference between game theory and mechanism design and identify the parts of a mechanism.

Learning Reflection

I learned that mechanism design starts with a desired outcome and asks what rules and incentives can make that outcome achievable.

Key Takeaways

  • Connect the day's concepts with strategic decision-making.
  • Use clear assumptions and structured reasoning when analyzing examples.
  • Review the topic and practice a related problem independently.
