Training Summary

We defined a dominant strategy as one that gives a player at least as good a payoff regardless of the other player's action, and a strictly dominated strategy as one that is always worse than another strategy. We practiced comparing payoffs across rows and columns and eliminating dominated strategies when appropriate.

Learning Objectives / Outcomes

Determine whether a strategy is dominant or dominated by comparing payoffs across all relevant cases.

Learning Reflection

I learned a systematic method for checking strategic choices instead of selecting a strategy based on only one possible opponent action.

Key Takeaways

  • Connect the day's concepts with strategic decision-making.
  • Use clear assumptions and structured reasoning when analyzing examples.
  • Review the topic and practice a related problem independently.
