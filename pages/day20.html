Training Summary

We explored applications of mechanism design in matching markets, resource allocation, public decision-making, and online platforms. The examples emphasized how rules determine who receives what, what information participants provide, and how incentives influence the outcome. We also discussed trade-offs among efficiency, fairness, and simplicity.

Learning Objectives / Outcomes

Connect mechanism-design concepts to real-world allocation and coordination problems.

Learning Reflection

The applications helped show that mechanism design is relevant to systems where people have different goals and private information.

Key Takeaways

  • Connect the day's concepts with strategic decision-making.
  • Use clear assumptions and structured reasoning when analyzing examples.
  • Review the topic and practice a related problem independently.
